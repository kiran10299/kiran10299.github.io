// about me

From hardware signals to intelligent software

I'm a Mechatronics engineer with 5+ years of experience designing, developing and deploying LabVIEW-based solutions. I've delivered automated test systems for organisations such as ISRO (SAC), NPCIL, Tata Power and LG India.

At Ducom Aerospace I build tribology test & measurement software that captures force, temperature, humidity, vibration and acoustic emission data. I also work on computer vision pipelines for human tracking and on ROS-based robotic mapping.

I like problems where real hardware meets software: controlling instruments over SCPI/GPIB, streaming sensor data over Modbus/CAN, and adding AI on top.

  • Test & MeasurementLabVIEW, ATE, DAQ, instrument control
  • RoboticsROS, Gazebo, RViz, SLAM
  • Computer VisionOpenCV, YOLO, MediaPipe, DeepSORT
  • Industrial CommsModbus, CAN, RS-485, HART, TCP/IP
